Manufacturing & Budget Planning · Week 7

Low-Cost FPCB Route for the Smart Wound Monitoring Patch

JLCPCB / PCBWay comparison · BOM · $20 budget analysis · fund request evidence
Supplier Comparison

JLCPCB, PCBWay, ALLPCB, NextPCB and LankaPCB were compared for flexible PCB fabrication and assembly feasibility. JLCPCB was identified as the most realistic low-cost bare-FPCB option.

Budget Finding

A strict 20 USD target is possible mainly for a bare FPCB route with coupon/cheap shipping. Full SMT assembly with TMP117 parts is expected to exceed the low-cost target.

Fund Request Preparation

The cost estimate, BOM, order process and supplier evidence were organized to support a clear fund request for the first prototype, including bare-board fabrication and essential sensor components.

End-of-Week 9 Disposable Patch Changes
No DAQ Electronics on Wound Contact

The expensive microcontroller/ADC/communication electronics were kept outside the disposable wound-contact patch to reduce cost and improve safety.

Exposed Sensing Windows Only

The patch direction was changed so only the pH and moisture sensing windows contact wound fluid; traces and connectors remain protected.

Reusable/Disposable Separation

The disposable layer carries low-cost sensing parts, while the reusable module handles data acquisition, processing and wireless transfer.

Safer Patch Materials

The design moved toward carbon/PANI sensing surfaces and protected interconnects rather than exposed copper or exposed electronics near the wound bed.
