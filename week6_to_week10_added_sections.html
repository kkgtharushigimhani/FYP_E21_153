Week 7
FPCB Cost Estimation, BOM Planning & Fund Request Preparation

Week 7 translated the prototype idea into a financial and manufacturing plan by comparing FPCB suppliers, estimating PCB/component costs, preparing a BOM, and organizing information needed for the project funding request.

Manufacturing & Budget Planning · Week 7

Low-Cost FPCB Route for the Smart Wound Monitoring Patch

JLCPCB / PCBWay comparison · BOM · $20 budget analysis · fund request evidence
Supplier Comparison

JLCPCB, PCBWay, ALLPCB, NextPCB and LankaPCB were compared for flexible PCB fabrication and assembly feasibility. JLCPCB was identified as the most realistic low-cost bare-FPCB option.

Budget Finding

A strict 20 USD target is possible mainly for a bare FPCB route with coupon/cheap shipping. Full SMT assembly with TMP117 parts is expected to exceed the low-cost target.

Fund Request Preparation

The cost estimate, BOM, order process and supplier evidence were organized to support a clear fund request for the first prototype, including bare-board fabrication and essential sensor components.
