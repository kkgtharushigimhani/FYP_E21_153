Week 08 · Patch Integration

Disposable Patch Integration & Manufacturing Challenges

Integrated pH, moisture and temperature sensors into a disposable strip concept.

EE405 Undergraduate Project IIWMTAS PortfolioWeek 08

Work completed

  • Designed a strip-like disposable patch so sterile wound-contact parts can be replaced while electronics are reused.
  • Included pH, moisture and temperature sensing windows with insulated routing and connector tail.
  • Documented manufacturing challenges such as biocompatible encapsulation, PANI uniformity and trace reliability after bending.

Key findings / design decisions

ChallengeDesign response
BiocompatibilityExpose only required sensing windows and insulate traces
PANI repeatabilityPlan calibration and repeatable coating process
Flex reliabilityUse careful trace routing and strain relief near connector
Relevance to IWMTAS: Integrated pH, moisture and temperature sensors into a disposable strip concept. This week strengthens the connection between clinical wound-healing evidence, sensor selection, practical patch fabrication and the final reusable electronics architecture.
