Week 05 · Biochemical Monitoring

pH Gradient in Wound Healing

Studied pH change from periwound/corner area toward the wound center.

EE405 Undergraduate Project IIWMTAS PortfolioWeek 05

Work completed

  • Extended the system beyond temperature by adding pH as a biochemical healing-condition indicator.
  • Studied how normal/periwound skin can be more acidic while chronic wound centers become more alkaline.
  • Linked alkaline wound-bed pH to delayed healing, bacterial activity, protease activity and possible infection risk.

Key findings / design decisions

RegionApproximate pH trendMeaning
Periwound / cornerpH 5.5–6.0Closer to normal skin barrier
Wound edgepH 6.2–6.8Active healing boundary
Wound centerpH > 7.1Possible delayed healing or infection risk
Relevance to IWMTAS: Studied pH change from periwound/corner area toward the wound center. This week strengthens the connection between clinical wound-healing evidence, sensor selection, practical patch fabrication and the final reusable electronics architecture.
