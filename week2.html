Week 02 · Sensor Method Study

Thermal Monitoring Methods & Wound Healing Biology

Compared temperature sensors and studied wound-healing physiology.

EE405 Undergraduate Project IIWMTAS PortfolioWeek 02

Work completed

  • Compared thermistors, RTDs, thermocouples, infrared sensors and digital IC sensors.
  • Reviewed hemostasis, inflammation, proliferation and remodeling stages of wound healing.
  • Confirmed that continuous thermal monitoring can track inflammation, perfusion and healing activity.

Key findings / design decisions

SensorReason
TMP117±0.1°C accuracy, I2C output, low power
NTC thermistorLow cost but calibration needed
